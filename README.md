CRC The Peridona: Exploring Luxury Living in Greater Noida
CRC The Peridona is a luxury residential development by CRC Group at Jaypee Greens, Greater Noida. The project brings together spacious residences, landscaped surroundings, lifestyle amenities and a design approach developed with a team of internationally recognised architecture, interiors, landscape and engineering consultants.
The project is registered with the Uttar Pradesh Real Estate Regulatory Authority under UPRERAPRJ298067/05/2025. The official RERA record identifies the project as The Peridona C-1 Jaypee Greens, promoted by CRC Greens Private Limited, with a declared completion date of 4 February 2030. Located at Plot C-01, Land-II, Integrated Sports Complex, Sector 26 and 31, Jaypee Greens, the development occupies a prominent residential location within Greater Noida.

 
What Defines CRC The Peridona?
CRC The Peridona has been planned around spacious residential living, with architecture, interiors, landscaping and shared facilities forming part of the overall development.
CRC Group's project information places emphasis on openness, natural light, greenery and carefully selected materials. These elements are intended to create a residential environment where the buildings and surrounding landscape work together rather than functioning as separate components.
The project also brings together an experienced design and consultancy team. CRC Group identifies Killa Design as the concept architect, Gensler as the resident architect, Rockwell Group for interiors and SWA Group for landscape design. CBM Engineers, Thornton Tomasetti and Mace are also associated with engineering and project management roles.
This design-led approach gives the project a distinct architectural identity while keeping the focus on the experience of the residences and shared spaces.
